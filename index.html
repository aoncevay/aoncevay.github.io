About

I'm an NLP researcher working on multilingual data, low-resource languages and evaluation. I hold a PhD in Informatics from the University of Edinburgh, where I studied how linguistic typology shapes neural machine translation.

I care about open science: shared tasks, public benchmarks and openly released data are how I've done most of my research, and I believe they're how the field gets better models for every language, not only the best-resourced ones.

Interests

Community & open research

  • AmericasNLP — co-founder & organiser, 2021–present Workshop series and open shared tasks on NLP for Indigenous languages of the Americas, with public benchmarks for languages that had none.
  • WMT · Translation with Terminologies — co-organiser, 2025–2026 Shared task on terminology-constrained translation: benchmark design, terminology data curation and system evaluation.
  • PUCP — external research advisor, 2018–present Supervising student research on long-tail and low-resource NLP at Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú.
  • ACL — area chair, 2025–present Reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, WMT, SIGTYP and LoResMT since 2019.
