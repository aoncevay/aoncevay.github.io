About

I'm an NLP researcher working on multilingual data, low-resource languages and evaluation. In industry (financial services), I focus on evaluating LLM and agentic systems for high-stakes applications, where reliability matters as much as raw capability. I hold a PhD in Informatics from the University of Edinburgh, where I studied how linguistic typology shapes neural machine translation.

Outside my day job, I'm committed to open research: through AmericasNLP, WMT shared tasks and my collaboration with PUCP, I help build public benchmarks and openly released data, so that better models are possible for every language, not only the best-resourced ones.

Community & open research

  • WMT · Translation with Terminologies — co-organiser, 2025–2026 Shared task on terminology-constrained translation: benchmark design, terminology data curation and system evaluation.
  • PUCP — external research advisor, 2018–present Supervising student research on long-tail and low-resource NLP at Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú.
  • ACL — area chair, 2025–present Reviewer for ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, WMT, SIGTYP and LoResMT since 2019.
